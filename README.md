<h1 align="center">Muses · Euterpe</h1>
<p align="center">A music home for your Windows desktop.</p>
<p align="center"><a href="https://xiaotwu.github.io/Project-Muses/">Explore Muses</a> · <a href="https://github.com/xiaotwu/Project-Muses/releases">Follow releases</a></p>

**Euterpe** is the Windows 11 edition of the [Muses family](https://github.com/xiaotwu/Project-Muses). It brings the product direction of Polyhymnia on the Mac to a native desktop experience, with artwork-led browsing and controls designed for Windows.

## Explore, organize, listen

- Browse songs, albums, artists and playlists.
- Keep your collection, favorites and listening history close.
- Control playback from a floating player and manage a persistent queue.
- Open Now Playing for cover or vinyl artwork and available lyrics.
- Watch video on demand and return to your listening session.
- Use Windows media controls and tray integration where available.

## At home on Windows

Desktop navigation, native window controls and Windows surface materials shape the experience. Euterpe follows the same product vision as the Mac edition while adapting to its own platform.

## Availability

Euterpe is **in development** for **Windows 11**. No downloadable Windows release or store package is currently published. Features and appearance are still evolving; complete parity with the Mac edition is not yet available.

Visit the [Muses website](https://xiaotwu.github.io/Project-Muses/) or follow [Releases](https://github.com/xiaotwu/Project-Muses/releases) for availability updates.
