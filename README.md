<h1 align="center">Muses · Euterpe</h1>
<p align="center">A music home for your Windows desktop.</p>

Euterpe brings the Muses experience to Windows with an interface based on Polyhymnia for Mac. Browse by artwork, keep your playlists and listening history close, and move from the floating player to the queue or an immersive Now Playing view.

Euterpe is in development. [Explore Muses](https://xiaotwu.github.io/Project-Muses/) · [Follow releases](https://github.com/xiaotwu/Project-Muses/releases)
